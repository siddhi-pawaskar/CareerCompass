ROADMAP

Decision-making timeline

Explore

List streams, careers, courses, and jobs without judging too early.

Assess

Compare strengths, marks, interests, budget, location, and support.

Validate

Talk to seniors, teachers, professionals, and counselors.

Act

Pick a path, build skills, and review progress every few months.

Common mistakes students make

Your strengths, learning style, and goals may be different from your friends.

Every path benefits from communication, digital literacy, problem solving, and discipline.

Most careers allow bridges, certifications, and future changes when planned carefully.

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Listen before advising.

  • Compare aptitude, interest, and real opportunities.

  • Encourage projects, reading, and career conversations.

  • Avoid using marks as the only measure of potential.
